Privacy Policy & POPIA Statement

Effective Date: October 2024 • Entity: Randomtech (Pty) Ltd • Germiston, Gauteng, South Africa

1 Responsible Party & POPIA Scope

This Privacy Policy governs the processing of personal information by Randomtech (Pty) Ltd ("the Responsible Party", "we", "us", or "our"), operating from Germiston, Gauteng, South Africa. We are committed to protecting your privacy in full compliance with the Protection of Personal Information Act, No. 4 of 2013 (POPIA), the Electronic Communications and Transactions Act, No. 25 of 2002 (ECTA), and international e-commerce privacy benchmarks (including Google Merchant Center requirements).

This policy applies to all visitors, buyers, registered users, and entities interacting with our digital storefront, web applications, customer service WhatsApp channels, or Direct EFT order management protocols.

2 Categories of Personal Information Collected

Under POPIA Section 1, "Personal Information" refers to information relating to an identifiable, living natural person or juristic entity. We collect only necessary personal data for clear, legitimate commercial operations, including:

Identity & Contact Data

Full legal name, business title, phone numbers, primary contact numbers, WhatsApp signatures, and official email addresses.

Logistics & Delivery Data

Physical delivery address across South Africa, postal code, suburb, gate access codes, and specialized offloading instructions for heavy freight.

Financial & Transaction Data

Order history, purchased items, Direct EFT payment references, Proof of Payment (POP) attachments, and digital tax invoices.

Technical & Audit Telemetry

IP addresses, browser environment, device OS signature, local storage session tokens, and security logs for control panel verification.

3 Lawful Condition & Purpose of Data Processing

In accordance with Section 11 of POPIA, personal data is processed lawfully and reasonably based on contractual necessity, legal obligations, or explicit user consent. We process data strictly for:

  • Order Execution & Dispatch: Validating transactions, preparing waybills, and dispatching kitchen appliances, solar panels, and water tanks via South African freight couriers.
  • Payment Verification: Matching Direct EFT payments against official bank statements before dispatching high-value solar equipment.
  • Communication & Support: Providing real-time delivery notifications and customer service via WhatsApp (`082 726 3274`) and email (`sales@randomtech.co.za`).
  • Regulatory & Tax Compliance: Maintaining statutory accounting records as mandated by the South African Revenue Service (SARS) and the Tax Administration Act.
  • Security & Anti-Fraud: Protecting store infrastructure and preventing unauthorized control panel access attempts.

4 Data Sharing & Third-Party Disclosure

Randomtech maintains a strict policy: we do not sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal information to marketing brokers or third parties. Data is disclosed only to verified service operators under strict confidentiality agreements:

  • Courier & Freight Partners: Licensed South African logistics carriers (e.g., The Courier Guy, Rhenus Logistics) receive only necessary name, contact, and address data to execute doorstep delivery.
  • Banking Institutions: First National Bank (FNB) / Standard Bank for verifying incoming Direct EFT deposits.
  • Legal Authorities: When compelled by valid subpoena, court order, or statutory requirement under South African law.

5 Data Retention & Storage Security

Personal data is retained only for as long as required to fulfill contract obligations or satisfy South African tax laws. Financial and tax transaction records are securely retained for a minimum of 5 to 7 years in compliance with SARS statutory requirements.

We employ physical, technical, and operational safeguards—including browser session isolation, brute-force lockout protections on administrative portals, and encrypted WhatsApp business channels—to protect against unauthorized access, loss, or damage.

6 Your Statutory Rights Under POPIA

Under POPIA Sections 23 and 24, as a Data Subject you hold statutory rights regarding your personal information:

• Right to Access: You may request confirmation of whether we hold personal information about you and request a copy of such record.

• Right to Rectification: You may request the correction or update of inaccurate, incomplete, or outdated personal information.

• Right to Erasure / Deletion: You may request the deletion or destruction of your personal data where retention is no longer authorized by law.

• Right to Object: You may object to data processing based on reasonable grounds or opt out of direct marketing communications at any time.
