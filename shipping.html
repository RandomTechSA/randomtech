1 Nationwide Delivery Coverage Across South Africa

Randomtech provides fast, reliable, insured doorstep delivery across all nine provinces of South Africa—including Cape Town, Johannesburg, Pretoria, Durban, Port Elizabeth, Bloemfontein, George, Nelspruit, Polokwane, and all regional or agricultural locations.

2 Free Delivery Qualification & Rate Structure

🎁 FREE Nationwide Delivery

Applies automatically to all store purchases with a cart order total of R1000 or more.

3 Transit & Dispatch Timelines

  • Dispatch Window: All verified orders (via Card, Instant EFT, or Direct EFT) are dispatched from partner fulfillment centers within 1 to 2 working days.
  • Courier Transit: Major metropolitan centers (Johannesburg, Pretoria, Cape Town, Durban) typically receive shipments within 1 to 3 working days post-dispatch. Regional and outlying areas require 3 to 5 working days.
  • Tracking: Tracking waybill numbers are generated instantly and dispatched via email and WhatsApp upon courier collection.
